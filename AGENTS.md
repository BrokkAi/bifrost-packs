# Working in this repository

- This repository is public. Review source licensing and Bifrost open-core
  classification before importing content from private repositories.
- Inspect the branch, worktree, dirty state, remotes, and relevant guidance
  before editing. Preserve unrelated changes.
- Keep stable policy and semantic-pack identities when migrating files.
- Preserve language and dependency scope, positive and realistic near-miss
  fixtures, and exact engine and semantic-model validation provenance.
- Keep incomplete and unsupported outcomes explicit. An empty result under
  incomplete coverage is not evidence of a clean program.
- Packaging and scanner enablement are separate from storing content here.
