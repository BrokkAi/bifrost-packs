Rust standard-library lock models

bifrost.rust-std-sync is original Brokk work under the Apache License,
Version 2.0. It states the lock-acquisition behavior of three public members
of the Rust standard library, std::sync::Mutex::lock, std::sync::RwLock::read
and std::sync::RwLock::write, and names them by the declaration identities of
bifrost.rust-stdlib (generated from the rustdoc JSON of nightly-2026-08-24,
rustc 1.100.0-nightly (fb6531d55 2026-08-23)). No standard-library source or
documentation text is copied. Rust is dual-licensed under the Apache License,
Version 2.0 and the MIT License.
