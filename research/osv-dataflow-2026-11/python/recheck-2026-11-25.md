### Why #271 still does not ship

The typeshed pack's `os.path` is a `from posixpath import *` re-export, so the
qualified locator `os.path.expandvars` resolves to nothing without an authored
declaration (`qualified-call-locator-zero`). The research models add `os.path`,
`posixpath` and `ntpath` module types and `expandvars` members. Promoting them
into `bifrost.python-stdlib-api-declarations` would duplicate typeshed's
`posixpath` identities. On 0.15.4 that makes other locators ambiguous, the
failure seen in #96 for `tempfile.mktemp`. Build the rule once an engine with
the bifrost-dev#14548 fix (listed for 0.15.5) is released, and check that the
certifying authored declaration wins. `posixpath.expandvars` also needs
bifrost-dev#10006.
